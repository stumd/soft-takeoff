How to play

  • Look closer. Click glowing markers, or anything that lights up under your cursor.
  • Move around. Drag to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom, double-click to reset.
  • Look closer. Tap anything in the list to read about it.
  • Let time pass when you’re ready. You don’t have to see everything, but things are only here once.
  • Visited moments stay on the timeline. Click a dot to go back.
  • Keys: Space let time pass · Esc close · M sound

Graphics
